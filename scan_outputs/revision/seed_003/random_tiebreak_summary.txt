Interpretation:
  Random tie-breaking removes T_occ's discrete-floor effect: under the
  conservative >= convention many T_occ ties stack at the high-rank end,
  which sharpens the achievable eps_B downward. Randomization spreads
  these uniformly, so T_occ operates closer to its nominal eps_B = alpha.
  The MATCHED-eps_B comparison (EXP-6) is unaffected; only the SIC AT
  nominal alpha = 1e-3 shifts for the discrete channel.
